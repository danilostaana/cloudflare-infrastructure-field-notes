# 10 — Client Offboarding

Offboarding should be planned during onboarding.

## Checklist

1. Confirm the client still has an active owner/admin.
2. Confirm billing is controlled by the client.
3. Transfer any client-owned documentation.
4. Transfer any client-owned code or configuration covered by the engagement.
5. Remove your API tokens from client automation where appropriate.
6. Rotate shared credentials if shared credentials existed.
7. Remove your Cloudflare membership when work is complete.
8. Remove your access from related hosting, Git, registrar, and monitoring systems.
9. Keep only records you are legally or contractually required to retain.
10. Record the offboarding date.

## Goal

After offboarding, the client should not depend on your personal account to operate its infrastructure.
