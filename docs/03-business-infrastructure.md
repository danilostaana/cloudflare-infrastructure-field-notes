# 03 — Business Infrastructure

## Purpose

Business Infrastructure contains assets owned by your business rather than you personally.

Examples:

- business website;
- company domains;
- production APIs;
- SaaS applications;
- business-operated services.

## Why separate it from personal infrastructure?

Separation helps with:

- ownership;
- accounting;
- billing;
- delegation;
- team access;
- future sale or transfer;
- continuity if another administrator joins.

## Access

Use named user accounts and scoped member permissions.

Avoid treating your personal login credentials as a shared business password.

Your user profile can still be an administrator of the business account without making the business resources part of your personal account.
